Security Awareness and Training Policy (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

1. Purpose
This policy establishes the security awareness and role-based training requirements for all
personnel with access to Example Machining information systems, including systems that store,
process or transmit Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

2. Scope
All employees, contractors and temporary staff.

3. Awareness of risk (all personnel)
3.1 Managers, system administrators and all users are made aware of the security risks
associated with their activities and of the applicable policies, standards and procedures
related to the security of company systems, at onboarding and annually thereafter.
3.2 Awareness content is reviewed each January by the Security Officer.

4. Role-based training
4.1 Personnel are trained to carry out their assigned information security related duties
and responsibilities before being granted privileged access, and annually.
4.2 System administrators complete the administrator security curriculum (SEC-TRN-02).
4.3 Completion is recorded in the LMS; records are retained for three years.

5. Insider threat
5.1 Annual awareness training includes recognizing and reporting potential indicators of
insider threat, with reporting through the Security Officer or the anonymous hotline.

6. Enforcement
Failure to complete required training results in suspension of system access until complete.

7. Review
This policy is reviewed annually and on significant change.
