# Information Security Policy (sample)

## Asset management

The company maintains an inventory of all enterprise assets, including laptops, servers, and endpoints. The IT team reviews and updates the asset inventory every quarter.

## Access control

All remote access and VPN connections require multi-factor authentication. Externally exposed applications must enforce MFA for every user before access is granted.

## Logging and monitoring

Audit logs are collected from servers and critical applications and retained for at least one year. Logging is enabled across systems to support detection and investigation of incidents.

## Backups

Automated backups of critical systems run nightly. Backup copies are kept offline so they survive a ransomware or destructive event, and restores are tested monthly.

## Acceptable use

Employees must not share passwords and must report lost devices to the IT team immediately.
